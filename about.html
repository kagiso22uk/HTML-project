MISSION

The mission of the department of mathematics and computer science is to be a dedicated and prominent center for delivering quality education in mathematics,applied mathematics,computer science and conducting impactful research that generates new ideas and innovative solutions relevant to society. The department is committed to excellence in research and education, producing adaptable graduates and providing knowledge and expertise for regional development. It is dedicated to the production of highly qualified,adaptable,self-directed,resourceful and multi-skilled graduates.


VISION

The vision of the department is to be a leading institution in mathematics and computer science education and research, contributing significantly to societal development and innovation.
